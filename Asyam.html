About Me

I was born in Kebumen on February 19, 2009. Growing up in a small town taught me that big dreams don't require big beginnings, only curiosity, consistency, and the courage to keep learning.

I understand how fast technology is developing today. For me, the rapid pace of technology and the limitations I face are not reasons to stop, but exciting challenges that make learning even more interesting.

Now I am a student at SMA Negeri 2 Purwokerto, focusing on Mathematics and Physics. My goal is to continue my studies in Electrical Engineering at Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB), and one day build a technology-based business that brings appropriate, affordable, and sustainable solutions to society.
